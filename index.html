FAQ

How big is the towel?

24×36 inches (about 60×90 cm), large enough to cover a whole bonnet or roof.

How much water does it hold?

Up to 1 gallon (about 3.8 litres), enough to dry a full car without wringing it out.

Will it scratch my paint?

No. The soft twisted loop microfiber is scratch-free and lint-free on paint and glass, even with heavy, repeated use.

How do I use it?

Lay it flat on the wet panel and pull it gently towards you. For best results, use it on a freshly rinsed car, out of direct sunlight.

How do I wash it?

As with all microfiber: wash separately, without fabric softener, and air dry or tumble dry on low heat.
